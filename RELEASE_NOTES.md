# Andy's Selective Grief Protection 0.1.7

Version 0.1.7 is the initial public release of the complete Selective Grief Protection add-on.

- Craft and place owned Protection Runes with square footprints, a fixed 20-block range above and below, and five upgrade tiers with radius caps from 16 through 256.
- **Crouch and interact directly with a placed rune to open its settings. No control stick is required.**
- Configure block placement, block breaking, containers, block interaction, item use, entity interaction, attacks against non-player entities, and optional PvP restrictions.
- Add trusted players as Visitor, Friend, Builder, Technician, Manager, or a custom permission set; transfer ownership to an online player when needed.
- Configure Creeper, Charged Creeper, Ghast, TNT, TNT Minecart, End Crystal, Wither, Wither Skull, Unknown, and administrator-defined Custom explosion sources.
- Choose All, Critical, Storage, Redstone, or Custom block filtering, per-dimension policies, six starting presets, and separate explosion entity-damage controls.
- Show a persistent illuminated boundary or request a 30-second preview. Toggle floating runes, the animated soul-campfire-style flame, and soul-flame sounds independently.
- Use `PlayerGriefControl` for personal feedback, warnings, preview preferences, and manageable-rune lists. Operators use `AdminGriefControl` for every world feature, source, dimension, limit, record, and custom-ID control.
- Administer Bedrock Dedicated Server worlds with the cheat-free `andys_sgp:admin` command, including settings, presets, rune inspection, enable/disable, transfer, stale-record removal, reload, and reset.
- Includes paired Behavior and Resource Packs, stable API dependencies, add-on product declarations, and PBR resources for Standard graphics and Vibrant Visuals. No experiments, beta APIs, or cheats are required.

Install both 0.1.7 packs together. Existing saved protection settings remain attached to the world.
